Find the sentences in a paper that read as AI-written.

Drop in a PDF. Jev reads every sentence, every paragraph and the paper as a whole, then marks each sentence as human written, AI assisted or AI generated, with how sure it is.

How Jev judges a paper

Every sentence

Asked three times: on its own, with the sentences before and after it, and inside its paragraph. The three answers are averaged into the highlight you see.

Every paragraph

Each paragraph is judged as one piece of writing, so a single odd sentence carries less weight.

The whole paper

The full text is judged at once. Long papers are split into as few parts as fit, and the parts are averaged.

The overall verdict is the average of those three scores. Your PDF stays on this machine; only its text is sent to Jev.
